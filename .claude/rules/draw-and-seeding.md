# Draw, seeding and pool distribution

Moved from CLAUDE.md "Key Algorithms". Read before changing how a draw places, seeds or numbers competitors.

- **Binary tree brackets** (`helper/tree.go`): `Node` struct with `Left`/`Right` children, recursive subdivision for multi-page output. `helper.MaxPlayersPerTree = 16` (constants.go).
- **Seeding** (`helper/seed.go`): `StandardSeeding()` uses `generateBracketOrder()` for placement. `ApplySeeds()` handles collisions by swapping seed values. Seed names and dojos are title-cased with `helper.TitleCaseName`, the one form every roster door stores a name and a dojo in (cases.Title(NoLower), operator decision D14 2026-11-01), then resolved by `domain.RosterIndex.LookupSeed`: by id when the row carries one, else exactly by (name, dojo), or by name alone when the row has no dojo and one roster player carries that name, so accents and non-initial capitals must match (NormalizeParticipantName is NOT on this path). A word starting with a digit is kept as typed, "2nd dojo" -> "2nd Dojo" (D22). A name an earlier release stored as "2Nd" keeps that spelling, since nothing lowers a stored letter (D23). A name it stored raw as "rin 2nd" reads "Rin 2nd" now, and the load gives the seeds and the unfought draw copies that spelling by id (D24 (b), `upgradeDrawSpellingLocked`; refer to store.md). `delayDojoMeetings` (same file) then delays dojo-mate meetings across the unseeded slots; its acceptance is SUM-DRIVEN by operator ruling (2026-08-31): a swap may pull an already-deep dojo's first meeting one round earlier to push the draw's earliest meetings later. Do NOT add a per-dojo never-earlier veto here: that rule belongs to the POOL exchange pass (`improveDojoMeetings`), and a finding that reports the deep-dojo trade as a regression is re-litigating the ruling.
- **Pool creation** (`helper/pool_distribution_tree_aware.go`): members are placed ONE AT A TIME in the roster's pre-shuffled order (no re-sorting -- shuffling is upstream's job), each descending the knockout skeleton tree by recorded per-node dojo counts (fewer of my dojo, then more room, then left); seeds are placed first on their EKC/D7 slots and recorded. A single pairwise exchange pass follows with a strictly tiered objective: per-pool spread-cap excess, winner-path round-1 count, winner-path meeting sum, then the all-qualifier crossing best effort -- each tier only resolves what the tier above left tied. No rotations. A crossed 2nd-place meeting a dojo-mate is ACCEPTED chance (operator ruling); never "fix" it beyond the tie-break tier. The old greedy fill's `assignPlayersToPools`/`discoverPool` survive only for `CreatePools` direct callers (estimator, goldens), where unique-dojo rosters make them identity-equal; `leastConflictedPool` is NOT one of those retirees -- it is load-bearing in the tree-aware distributor itself, called from three sites (`pickDojoTreeAwarePool`'s two fallbacks plus `poolUnderDojoCap`) as the descent's own least-conflicted fallback when the dojo tree has nothing to say. Pools distributed contiguously across shiaijo. **Competitor numbers belong to draw positions, never to people before a draw exists** (operator ruling 2026-09-06): once the entrant count fixes the pools and the tree, the positions are numbered pool by pool (`helper.NumberPools`, K1-K4 the first pool) and seeds and everyone else are placed into those positions; nothing is shown before the draw is generated (the earlier provisional registration-order numbers were removed as confusing). A knockout-only competition follows the SAME rule down the bracket instead of pool by pool (bc-pnum ruling 2, 2026-09-07): `helper.StandardSeeding`'s own placement order is recorded as `state.Bracket.DrawOrder` (participant ids, top of the tree to the bottom) when the draw is generated, and `engine.NumberKnockoutParticipants` composes each competitor's number from their position in it at read time; a competitor excluded from the draw (e.g. not checked in), or a bracket generated before this field existed, carries no number -- there is no participant-order or name-based fallback. **Changing any of this reddens a test that names a docs file**: the pool-draw page carries an interactive walk-through (`docs/assets/javascripts/pool-draw-animation.js`) that replays this descent in JS, and its example rosters' drawn pools are a fixture between markers in that same file, pinned by `TestPoolDrawDocWalkthroughMatchesTheDraw` (`internal/helper`) re-running them through the real distributor. That is deliberate: a recorded outcome rots silently otherwise. Update the fixture, then re-check the JS descent against it in a browser, since the test pins the recorded pools and not the JavaScript.
- **Tree-aware seeding** (`helper/seed.go`): `placeSeedIndices`, called from `buildPoolPhaseTreeAwareCore` (`helper/pool_distribution_tree_aware.go`; `PoolSeeding` itself was removed as dead code, bc-drwx item 11), places seeds so the top seeds land in different halves/quarters of the knockout TREE (D7, enforced via tree paths in seed_warnings.go); the EKF rank-to-court order is that tree contract's sheet encoding, since courts own contiguous chunks of the tree. Courts are NOT an overlay here: `numCourts` is the modulus `seedPoolRank`/`seedCourtOrder` compute the seed spread against, so a wrong court count places seeds in the wrong quarter. (Courts genuinely are an overlay for the SEPARATE dojo-tree descent that places the unseeded -- see the Pool creation bullet above -- which reads a court index only to derive `drawCourts` up front and to reorder for courts at the very end, never in between.) **Wrapped seeds** (rank beyond `numPools`, e.g. rank 5 over 4 pools -- reachable from a contiguous seeding with more ranks than pools, or from check-in dropping a mid-sequence seed and leaving a gap, `TestGenerateDraw_SeedGapFromCheckInKeepsD6Placement`): the extra seed takes the next pool (in the same rank-priority order the natural arithmetic defines) with room holding NO seed; failing that, the next with room holding no DOJO-MATE; only once both fail does it fall through to D6's unconditional placement. Once placed, a wrapped seed is never moved by anything downstream -- not the pool exchange pass (`improveDojoMeetings`, which excludes every seeded slot from both sides of a swap) and not `delayDojoMeetings` (which only ever moves unseeded slots, via its own `occupied` guard). `seed_warnings.go`'s halves/quarters/shiaijo checks are scoped to ranks `<= maxSeedRanks` for the same reason: a wrapped seed has no half/quarter home in D6's structure, so scoring it against those checks by its COUNT position in the placed-seeds list (rather than by its rank value) produced a spurious "could not be split into halves" warning for a wrapped seed that had landed cleanly in its own seed-free pool (bc-pnum operator ruling; restores what an earlier review round had narrowed to the dojo-mate case alone).
- **The draw review: dojo separation, swaps and suggestions, all before the first result** (bc-drev, operator decisions D1 to D8, 2026-11-25; P0: "dojo summary first after the draw. The swaps should be suggested as a separate step"). Three owners, none stored, each recomputed from the stored draw on every call (like the seed warnings), so none can go stale against a changed draw:
  - **`engine.DojoSeparation`** (`internal/engine/dojo_separation.go`, pure; `DojoSeparationFor` loads and calls it) is the ONE rule for "where can two dojo-mates first meet". Competitors are grouped by `helper.DojoKey` (exported for this: the key the draw placed them by), and a blank or whitespace dojo forms no group. A shared pool is `samePool` with no meeting; two pools give `ifBothWin` (the two 1st places) and `earliest`, taken over EVERY qualifying place the bracket holds for the two pools, read from the bracket's draw-time `PlaceholderA`/`PlaceholderB` labels, so extra qualifiers count and the answer is the same before and after the pools finish; a knockout-only draw gives the meeting of the two first-round leaves (the bracket is position encoded: leaves i and j meet in `Rounds[k-1][i>>k]` at the first k where `i>>k == j>>k`). A side carries `number` only when the competition numbers: pools.csv's `Number` for a pools draw, `NumberKnockoutParticipants` over `DrawOrder` for a knockout (the one derivation; no `DrawOrder`, no number). A league, a Swiss competition and a competition with no draw answer an empty list; a draw that cannot be read is an ERROR, never an empty list (which would say no dojo-mates meet early). The swap's dry run and the suggestions call the same pure function on in-memory copies, so all three answer by one rule.
  - **`engine.SwapDrawPlaces`** (`draw_swap.go`) trades two competitors' places in ONE `WithTransaction`, every refusal judged under the lock, worked out by the pure `planSwap` that the dry run (`dryRun: true`) answers unsaved, so "If you swap them" cannot drift from the real swap. Refusals, in that order: `swap_not_supported` (league, Swiss), `no_draw`, `draw_started` (not draw-ready, a recorded result by `recordedResultIn`, or `foughtMatch`: any bout row, score, history or score-log file in a still-draw-ready competition, a guard that should never fire since any start leaves draw-ready), then `invalid` (a place that is not exactly one shape), `same_competitor` (so a request naming one competitor twice answers this even when both places are equal), `invalid` again (two different competitors naming one place), `not_in_draw` (the sentence says whether the id is off the roster or was not drawn), and `draw_changed` (carrying `places`, where both are now). The API answers `invalid`, `same_competitor` and `not_in_draw` 400 and the rest 409 (`swapRefusalStatus`, mobileapp/errors.go). A resend that finds each competitor at the OTHER's named place is 200 `applied:false, reason:"already_swapped"` and changes nothing. A place is `{pool, position}` (1-based) for a pools draw or `{slot}` (0-based first-round leaf, side A of match m is 2m) for a knockout-only draw. `exchangePlaces` applies ONE two-way id map in a single pass, by id only: pools.csv's two rows trade the competitor (id, name, display name, dojo, seed) and KEEP position and `Number` (decision D2 (b): "tags are attached to the number and not the player name"), pool-matches.csv sides and winners take the other's id and name, and every bracket slot (`forEachBracketSlot`) is rewritten: sides A and B through `seatBracketSide`, the Winner (a bye the draw resolved) written DIRECTLY, name and id, since `seatBracketSide` writes sides only and logs a BUG for the Winner slot. `DrawOrder` trades the two ids, so `NumberKnockoutParticipants` and the load repair `StampRoundZeroSideIDsFromDrawOrder` stay right, and `RenumberCompetitors` needs no change (it numbers by position, which the place keeps). Courts, times, match ids and placeholder labels never move. No history line is written: nothing was fought, and competitor-status records and check-ins are keyed by participant id, so they stay with the competitor.
  - **A seed may be moved, with a warning** (decision D1 (b)). `seededWarning` names each seeded competitor the swap moves out of their pool or knockout place. In a pools + knockout draw such a swap REBUILDS the knockout preview from the swapped pools with the draw's own builder, unchanged: `buildPoolPreviewBracket` (bracket.go) is the pure build split out of `generatePoolPreviewBracket`, which stays the draw's and the quarantine rebuild's wrapper (build, then save), and the court floor (`courtFloorFor`) and the tournament are read BEFORE the transaction takes the lock (`swapPreviewBuilder`), as the draw reads them; a read that fails refuses only a swap that rebuilds. `keepBracketSchedule` is the schedule rule: when every match id is a match to play (or a bye) both before and after, each match keeps its stored court, time and queue place; when any id flips between bye and match, or appears or goes, the builder's WHOLE new schedule is taken, never a mix (which could put two matches on one court at one time), and the answer says `timesRescheduled`; change stamps are kept either way. A swap inside one pool, a swap of two unseeded competitors, and every knockout-only swap rebuild nothing (`bracketRebuilt` false).
  - **A stale write after a swap is refused by the existing side check**, not by a draw version: a start or a result (a queued offline one included) from a device that loaded the match before the swap names the old pairing, so the engine refuses it `ErrMatchSideMismatch` (`side_mismatch`), writes nothing, and the competition does not start (`TestSwapDrawPlaces_StaleWritesAreRefused`, pool and bracket, a start by name only included). A decision (kiken, fusenpai, fusensho, both-barred hikiwake) names a side by colour alone, so it carries the participant ids its writer saw (`DecisionRequest.sideAId`/`sideBId`, `ForceOptions.Seen` (`engine.SeenSides`), read from the match by `seenSideIds` in `competitor_identity.jsx`) and `recordDecisionTx` refuses one whose ids differ from the stored pairing, the same `side_mismatch` (`TestSwapDrawPlaces_StaleDecisionIsRefused`, `TestDecisionHandler_StaleSides_409`). A side the writer sent no id for is not judged. Do not bump match stamps for this: a decision made after the swap on a stale screen is stamped after it.
  - **`engine.SuggestSwaps`** (`draw_swap_suggestions.go`; `SwapSuggestionsFor` loads) offers swaps of two UNSEEDED competitors in different pools (or different first-round matches), never a seed, since moving one rebuilds the bracket. **A swap is judged ONE DOJO AT A TIME, never pair by pair** (operator decision C1, 2026-11-26): for each dojo, the `rankMeeting` of each of its pairs before and after, each list sorted earliest first, compared from the earliest; the first difference decides (later is better off, earlier worse off, equal lists unchanged). The swap is `worse` when ANY dojo is worse off (a trade between two dojos stays worse), `better` when none is and one is better off, `same` otherwise, so two dojo-mates trading places is `same`, and a dojo whose list gets later is better off though one of its pairs meets earlier. `judgeDojos` (`dojoTally.shift`) is the ONE owner of that rule for the suggestions and the candidates alike, and the browser's `swapVerdict` (`separationChanges`, admin_draw_swap_check.jsx) words the check with the same rule; do not reintroduce a per-pair verdict beside it. `rankMeeting` is the browser's `meetingLateness` exactly (rounds counted from the final, by `DisplayRound` when set; no if-both-win meeting breaks no tie), because sorting a dojo's meetings needs both to order them alike. A candidate is offered only when `judgeDojos` calls it better (a shared pool is earliest, then earlier rounds, the if-both-win meeting breaking a tie); its `improves` are the pairs of the dojos better off that meet later; ranked by the earliest meeting it fixes, then how much later, then how many pairs improve, then ids; at most `maxSwapSuggestions` (5). The answer is `suggestions` alone (the old `stuck` list is gone: the card's conclusion line says "No swap can do better." itself). Each suggestion carries `byes`, the first-round byes it hands over. Without a rebuild only the two competitors' own pairs can change, so a candidate is scored on those alone from a per-dojo index (a 256-competitor draw stays well under a second, `TestSwapSuggestions_TimingFor256`); the incremental score is pinned to `DojoSeparation` on `planSwap`'s result by an oracle test (`TestSwapSuggestions_GeneratedDrawsMatchTheSummary`), so change both or neither. This per-dojo no-dojo-worse-off rule binds the SUGGESTIONS and the swap marks alone: the draw's `delayDojoMeetings` stays sum-driven by the 2026-08-31 ruling above, so do not port the veto into the draw. An empty list is the usual, correct answer, because the draw already optimises dojo meetings; the card's conclusion line then says so. The card offers no pickers of its own: its **Swap competitors on Pools** (or **on the Bracket**) button opens the preview tab in the swap mode through `swap_mode_request.jsx` (`askSwapMode`, taken once by `useSwapCompetitors` as the tab mounts), whose banner is pinned under the topbar and publishes `--swap-banner-h`.
  - **`engine.SwapCandidatesFor`** (`draw_swap_candidates.go`, operator decision K4 (c), `GET .../draw/swap-candidates?id=`) marks, for the competitor picked first in the swap mode, every other drawn competitor (seeded or not, same pool or not, in draw order) `worse`, `better` or `same` by `judgeDojos` (each dojo as a whole, above; another match of the same round is not earlier). It reads the draw in ONE `WithTransaction` like the dry run and refuses as the swap does (`swappableDraw`, shared with `planSwap`: `swap_not_supported`, `no_draw`, `draw_started`), with `not_in_draw` for a pick the draw does not hold (409 here, 400 on the swap). A swap that moves no seed is scored by the suggester's `pairShifts`, which `evaluate` also uses; a swap that moves a seed runs the swap's own `exchangePlaces` and `seededSwap`, and when that rebuilds the knockout the WHOLE separation after it is compared (`separationVerdict`, per dojo by `helper.DojoKey`), since any pair can move. Pinned against the real dry run with the browser's per-dojo reading ported (`browserVerdict`, `TestSwapCandidates_AgreeWithTheSwapDryRun`), and the three paths against each other on hand-built draws (`TestSwapVerdict_EachDojoAsAWhole`). A seeded pick in a 256-competitor pools draw rebuilds once per candidate, about 1.2 s (`TestSwapCandidates_TimingFor256`).
  - **The first recorded result fixes the draw** (decision D4 (b)): from then on a swap is `draw_started`, because a fought match must never change sides. The browser asks before that first result (`first_result_confirm.jsx`; see `.claude/rules/match-writes.md`); the server never gates a write on that question.
- **A pool bout's number is its place in the playing order, and the id carries it** (operator ruling 2026-10-04: "Should be in playing order and both ids should be in sync"). `numberPoolMatchesInPlayingOrder` (engine/pools.go) gives each pool's ids after every reorder the draw makes (the league scheduler's no-back-to-back order), just before the save, so `Pool A-0` is the first bout played. Every surface reads the number from the id: the server label below, the scores list, the workbook (`AttachPoolMatches` sorts by it) and the console's "Match N of M" (`poolPosition`, `poolMatchNumberOf`, viewer_utils.jsx), never from a row position. A queue reorder moves only `scheduledAt`, so a bout keeps its number. A league drawn by an earlier release keeps ids given before its reorder; every surface still shows one number for each of its bouts.
- **The tie rule: of the podium places, only 3rd can be shared** (operator rulings, rounds rule-1 to rule-3). In a league (team or individual, Engi included) `leagueTiebreakBand` (3, `internal/engine/league_tiebreak.go`) is a constant, not a setting; `isConsequentialTie` is its one judge. A tie at 1st or 2nd, and at 3rd when `EffectiveTwoThirdPlaces()` is off, gets a tie-breaker with a winner; at 3rd with joint 3rd places on, and at 4th or lower, the place is shared and nothing is asked or played. There is no Accept shared ranks and no Undo (the finalize routes, `LeagueTiebreakFinalized` and `LeagueTiebreakTopN` are gone); the only way to take back an unfought tie-breaker is `DELETE .../league-tiebreak`. Lots (`chusen.go`, the organiser draws and enters them, the app never ranks a cycle) are offered only for a cycle a round of bouts cannot separate, or when both sides of the tie-breaker are barred and the bout was recorded as drawn (`Record as drawn (neither can fight)`, `barredDrawRuns`). That drawn bout settles nothing until the organiser either enters the lots or fights it again (the drawn-bout notice's reopen; it waits on its court until both can fight, which may be never since only a kiken-injury is reinstated, and the court can record it drawn again meanwhile), and the app forces neither (operator ruling 2026-11-16, "We should not be enforcing a path"); the same holds in pools + knockout, for an ippon-shobu and a representative bout alike, and `POST /complete` answers `barred_tiebreak_draw_pending` naming both (a league names a circle owed beside it first). A both-barred draw whose two sides the fought bouts already put each in a run of its own decides no place and settles as it stands (`drawnBarredDecidesNoPlace`). Pools keep their own rule: a tie gets a bout only when it decides who goes through or where they are seated (`tieAffectsAdvancement`). Engi pools and leagues follow the same rule, the tie-break bout scored by flags (`engiStandingPoints` marks the level pairs; a flag bout cannot end level), and name order never decides a place that counts; Swiss does not follow the rule yet (its order is wins, ippons or an Engi pair's flags, head-to-head, then name). `config.md` keys `league_tiebreak_finalized` and `league_tiebreak_top_n` are dropped by the legacy upgrade, and such a league reopens on its next result, withdrawal, reinstatement or lots change.
