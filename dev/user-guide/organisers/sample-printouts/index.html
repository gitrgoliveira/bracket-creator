Sample printouts

These are the PDFs printed for two example competitions, so you can see what each one looks like before you print your own. Export PDFs in the tournament app and the print command print the same sheets. Select a picture to open the whole PDF.

Each picture shows one page; the text above it says what the rest of that PDF holds.

Individual competition

24 competitors in pools of three, the first two of each pool going through to a knockout, on two shiai-jo. Printed from the example workbook pools-example-medium.xlsx.

Registration sheet

The list of competitors with their pool, name, dojo, zekken name and number. A4.

Name sheets

A3 landscape. After a title page, each shiai-jo's sheets open with this page: the competition's name three times, to cut out and put on the scoreboard. The pages after it hold the competitors' numbers and names, three to a page.

Competitor tags

Each competitor's number, printed large, one page for each competitor. A4. A tag also carries a QR code when the tournament's public URL is set; this sample has none.

Pools and trees

A booklet for competitors and coaches: the pool draw, then each shiai-jo's knockout tree with the pools beside it. A4.

Full bracket

Everything the tables need to run the day on paper: the pool draw, every pool's matches with a results table, every knockout match, and the trees. A4.

Team competition

12 teams of five in pools of three, the first two of each pool going through to a knockout, on two shiai-jo. Printed from the example workbook pools-example-medium-teams.xlsx. There are no competitor tags: tags are printed for individual competitors only (with print, a workbook counts as a team one when its name contains team or it is named with --team-file).

Registration sheet

The list of teams with their pool, dojo and number. A4.

Name sheets

A3 landscape, with the competition's name three times after the title page, like the individual competition's. The pages after it hold the teams' numbers and names.

Pools and trees

The pool draw, then each shiai-jo's knockout tree with its pools beside it. A4.

Full bracket

The pool draw, every team match with a numbered row for each of its five bouts, every knockout match, and the trees. A4.
